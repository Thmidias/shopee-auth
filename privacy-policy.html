PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION STANDARD

Movliv - Moda Fitness Oficial
Effective Date: June 15, 2025

1. Introduction and Scope

Movliv - Moda Fitness Oficial ("Movliv", "we", "our") is committed to protecting the personal information of users, customers, and partners. This Personal Information Protection Standard ("Standard") establishes the principles, policies, and procedures governing the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of personal data in connection with our e-commerce operations, including integrations with third-party platforms such as TikTok Shop.

This Standard applies to all personal data processed by Movliv in the course of its business activities, including data collected through online storefronts, APIs, and partner platform integrations.

2. Types of Personal Information Collected

2.1 Customer Information

  • Full name
  • Delivery address and billing address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Order history and transaction data
  • Payment method details (processed exclusively through platform-level secure payment gateways)

2.2 Platform Integration Data

  • Seller account identifiers (Shop ID, Partner ID)
  • API access tokens (encrypted, stored securely, not shared with third parties)
  • Order and logistics data exchanged with partner platforms

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Personal information is collected and processed solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Order fulfillment and logistics management
  • Customer service and communication
  • Fraud prevention and account security
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations
  • Platform integration and API functionality (e.g., TikTok Shop Open Platform)

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Protection and Security Measures

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • All API credentials and access tokens are stored as encrypted environment variables and never hardcoded in source code
  • HTTPS/TLS encryption is used for all data transmission
  • API communications use HMAC-SHA256 signature verification to prevent unauthorized access
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • API tokens are rotated periodically and immediately upon any suspected compromise

4.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Access to personal data is limited to personnel who require it to perform their duties
  • All personnel handling personal data are informed of their data protection responsibilities
  • Vendor and partner integrations are reviewed for compliance before activation

5. Data Breach Notification

Movliv maintains a data breach response procedure that includes:

  • Immediate containment of the breach upon detection
  • Assessment of the scope and nature of the breach within 24 hours
  • Notification to affected users and relevant platform partners within 72 hours of confirmed breach identification, where required by applicable law or platform policy
  • Documentation of all breach incidents and corrective actions taken

We are committed to transparency and prompt action in the event of any security incident involving personal data.

6. User Rights

Individuals whose personal data we process have the following rights, subject to applicable law:

  • Right to access their personal information
  • Right to correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion of personal data, where legally permissible
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

Requests may be submitted to: thiagocarvalho111@gmail.com

7. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Standard, or as required by applicable law. Order and transaction data is retained for a minimum of 5 years for legal and accounting compliance. API credentials are invalidated and deleted upon termination of the platform integration.

8. Third-Party Platform Compliance

Movliv operates in compliance with the terms, policies, and developer guidelines of all integrated platforms, including the TikTok Shop Open Platform Developer Agreement. We acknowledge and adhere to the data protection requirements set forth by these platforms and cooperate fully with any compliance review or audit.

9. Policy Updates

This Standard may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our operations, legal requirements, or platform policies. The effective date at the top of this document will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our platform integration constitutes acceptance of the updated Standard.
